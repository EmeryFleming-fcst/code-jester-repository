- Sound Track Elements:
-- Dialogue
-- Music
-- Sound Effects
-- Foley
-- Ambience
- Ambient Effects: Static effects like wind, birds, traffic, flowing waters, trees, roomtones, etc.
-- They are not tied to an object.
- Sound Effects: Tied to an object and are pannable.
-- Dynamic Sounds. Specific doors, cars, horns, birds, and almost anything you can see on screen.
- Ask:
-- What time of day is it?
-- What obvious sounds should I put?
-- What other sounds can I add?
-- Time Period?
-- Temperature?
-- Wild life?
-- Location in the world?
- Process:
-- Works in 5.1
-- Start with the BGs
--- Fill stereo channels with wind, distant insects, birds.
--- Fill out Mono Channels with mid-range wind and grass movement.
--- Fill out Surrounds with static mid-range wind and just some grass movement.
-- Add some sound effects
--- Hammering guy. Push Back.
--- Focus on Trees
-- Automated Wind Roar
-- Sugar
--- Add some Generic Birds
--- Add a crow pan
--- Add grasshopper to show warmth
