-- Oversampling:
--- Find the Nyquist Frequency by halfing the sample rate. The highest frequency that can be accurately recorded and reproduced.
--- Saturation can go above that frequency threshold.
--- Aliasing: Bounces harmonics back down and they can be undesirable.
--- Oversampling prevents aliasing but increases latency by a lot. 4x is usually enough, 8x is driving really hard.
