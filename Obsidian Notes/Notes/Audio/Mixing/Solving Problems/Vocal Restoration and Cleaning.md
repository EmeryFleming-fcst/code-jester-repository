1. When using Izotope, refer to the [[Vocal Processing.png]] flowchart for assistance. See [[RX11]] for a detailed breakdown of the modules.
2. Simple problems and their solutions:
	1. Problem: Is the vocal too breathy?
		1. Use the RX Breath Control Module
		2. Automate the breath and lower the gain of it.
	2. Problem: Is there audio bleed?
		1. Use RX De-Bleed with the track that bled into the audio.
	3. Problem: Is there clicking?
		1. Use RX De-Click.
		2. Attempt to manually cut them and crossfade. 
		3. Attempt to remove them with gain automation.
	4. Problem: Is there clipping?
		1. Use RX De-Clip
	5. Problem: Is there crackling?
		1. Use RX De-Crackle
	6. Problem: Is the sibilance too strong?
		1. Use RX De-Ess
		2. Use a different De-Ess plugin
		3. Lower the gain of the sibilance
		4. Use Melodyne. Copy the track with Melodyne on it, turn one into only consonants and the other to only sibilance. Lower the sibilance in the other track as needed.
		5. Use EQ on the affected frequency.
		6. Use Dynamic EQ to respond to the sibilance on the affected frequency.
	7. Problem: Is there a persistent background noise, like an AC?
		1. Use RX De-Hum BEFORE Voice or Spectral repair.
	8. Problem: Are there Plosives?
		1. Use RX De-Plosive.
		2. Cut the affected area and use crossfading to attempt to manage it.
	9. Problem: Is there reverb?
		1. Use RX De-Reverb.
	10. Problem: Is there a low signal-to-noise ratio?
		1. Use RX Dialogue Isolate.
	11. Problem: Are there mouth clicks?
		1. Use RX Mouth De-Click
	12. Problem: Is there background noise that is stationary or slow to change?
		1. Use RX Spectral De-Noise
	13. Problem: Are there undesired sounds in the audio?
		1. Use RX Spectral Repair directly on the spectrogram.
	14. Problem: Is there background noise behind the vocal?
		1. Use RX Voice De-Noise
