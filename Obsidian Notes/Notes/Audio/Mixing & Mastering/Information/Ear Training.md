### Ear Training Method [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_VRsYNX5RdM)
- Train your ears a little bit every day
- Frequency is key to good ear training, not length.
- Try to keep is quick and consistent
- Methodology:
	1. Do daily drills
		- Sound Gym
		- Audio Drillz
		- EQ Academy Plugin
	2. Analyze a Track
		- Categories to record:
			- Track
				- Which track you picked?
			- Tone
				- What's bright?
				- What's dark?
				- Is the track overall bright or dark?
				- Where are things positioned?
			- Balance
				- How is the Volume Balance?
				- How is the Panning?
				- What is up-front?
				- What is in the back?
				- Where are things panned?
			- Dynamics
				- How punchy is it?
				- How compressed?
			- Space
				- What kinds of reverb are used?
				- Where are delays used?
				- Is the mix wet or dry?
				- How is space used?
				- What is the sense of depth?
			- Notes
				- What observations did you make?
