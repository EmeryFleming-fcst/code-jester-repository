### Simple Mixing Workflow [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKrF2dra1S0)
1. Session Prep
	- Organize Tracks, Group similar tracks together, Color-coding, Labeling song sections, Gain Staging, Selecting Reference Tracks, Setting Buffer Size to it's highest limit.
2. Initial Balance
	- Faders and Panning. Get the best mix you can with just those.
3. Mix Bus
	- Add effects to here first.
4. Process Top down
	- Process Parent busses first, then process each individual channel as needed.
	- How is the tone? -> How are the dynamics? -> Does it need space?
5. Listen throughs
	- Listen through everything, beginning to end. Take notes on what needs to be fixed. Make Revisions. 3-5 Rounds.
6. Evaluate Translation
	- Make sure it sounds well on any system.
	- Use a Frequency Analyzer to check how closer yours is to the reference.
	- Add EQ at the end of Mix Bus Chain to nudge it as close as you can.
	- Choose any 3 Speaker Systems and play your mix in a playlist next to the reference track. Note any red flags. Phone, Bluetooth Speaker, Earbuds, etc.
### Mixing Tips [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-tI7kEC6Blo)
1. Do whatever you want to your aux channels.
2. Don't solve problems that don't exist.
3. Be intentional.
4. Mixing is iterative and dynamic.
5. Use References.
6. Use Parallel Processing.
7. Take lots of breaks.
8. Use Automation.
9. EQ the aux channels.
10. Get it right at the source.
11. Create Front and Back Depth.
12. Check it in Mono.
13. Use multiple monitors.
14. Room treatment is more important than monitors.
15. You're selling your taste, not you're skills.
16. Mixing is not finishing the production, it's adding to it.
17. Study your hero's influences, not your hero.
18. Stop taking advice so literally.
19. Work with the people in your "neighborhood."
20. Teacher are the best resource we have.
21. Mix other people's tracks.
22. Deadlines are vital. They provide commitment.
23. Know why the rough mix can be better.
24. Aim to capture feelings when mixing.
25. Rules are made to be ignored.
