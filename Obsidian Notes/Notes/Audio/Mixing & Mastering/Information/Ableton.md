### 5 Ableton Settings [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Sy1Ev2VMig)
- Create Fades on Clip Edges off.
- Right click track. Quantize settings. Turn off end.
- Reduced Latency when Monitoring. Make this default session.
- Buffer Size: Lower you go, the lower the latency.
- Warp Settings: 
	- Beats Warp preserves transients.
	- Complex 
		- Complex Pro: Best for changing the tempo. Vocals.
		- Higher the Formant, the closer to the original vocals.
- Repitch: Changes the pitch.
- Default Presets for Plugins: Right-click. Can do the same for audio/midi tracks.

### 12 Life-Changing Ableton Tips [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SZ3PTkgy2aA)
- Create an empty midi-track called length that covers the length of the whole track. Use to keep your place and to render entire length easier.
- Use search to find things.
- Get rid of Empty space when cutting. Select all, CTRL-X, paste in session view tracks slots, cut again, paste back in normal view.
- Options -> Reduced Latency when Monitoring.
- Hover over title bar of plugin. Latency appears in bottom left corner.
- Shift + Tab: Swaps between track and plugin view.
- Ctrl + 1, 2, 3, 4
- Ctrl + LIMBO
	- L: Launch Panel
	- : inputs and outputs
	- M: Mixer
	- B: Browser
	- O: Overview
- Warp in the timeline: Shift + Drag at the top bar of a clip.
- Slipping: Shift + ALT + Drag. Move position in clip around.
- Add Ctrl to warp and slip to snap grid on.
