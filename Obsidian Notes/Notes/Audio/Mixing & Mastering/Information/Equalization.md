## Tips
---
### Low-end not cutting through the mix? [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pGtnRIyusA)
- Use a Shelf EQ to boost everything BUT the low end. 
- Fletcher-Munson Curve.
### EQ Balance Chart [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wrUpFF7nlOg)
- If you're just starting out, use a balance chart.
- Seesaw Effect: Sometimes if you hear a problem on one end of the spectrum, the actual solution may be on the other end.
### Stop High Passing Everything [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bJrPtXYNtI)
- Use a shelf instead of always high/low passing
- You can retain some of the harmonics by limiting it
