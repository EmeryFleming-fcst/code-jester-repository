### Getting Weird with Parallel Compression [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DRkMvuNZ8_E)
- Use Saturation after a Compressor on a parallel compressed track to add flavor.
	- Normal Track + Compressed Track + Compressed Track with Saturation
- Use Modulation
	- Add Width plugin and modulate after compressor
- Use Pitch Shifting
	- Add to the parallel compressed channel
