- Sound Field
	- Stereo Imager
	- Phase Meter: +1 is good phase polarity. 0 - +1 is in phase. Anything in-phase should fall within that angle.
	- Polar Level: Sound rays. Length display amplitude.
	- Lissajous: Pretty tall, not as wide as it's tall, indicates good phase.
	- History: Shows which parts are quiet/loud over a period of time.
	- Spectrum: Shows full scope of frequency in mix. Zoom in. Enable Peak Hold in settings. Time vs Frequency. 3D. Use Relay to listen to different parts of track. Can compare to instrument groups.
