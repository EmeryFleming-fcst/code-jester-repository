### Dynamics [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6BDYnPwpLJs)
- Can increase volume of quiet notes and decrease volume of loud notes. Based on selection.
- Can use amplify to increase the volume of a note. Sometimes you lift the formants a bit.
### Pitch Drift [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WfMBaE3XX6Q)
- Typically, the longer the notes, the higher the need for this tool. Try to center pitch.
- Tip: If the pitch moves clearly between two semitones:
	- Separation tool.
	- Pitch the note together.
	- Use Pitch transition tool. Make it as horizontal as possible.

### Melodyne preserves emotion and soul in vocals [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Oy-nClro-s)
- Transitions
- When doing massive note shifts, drag the formant back down a bit for smoothness.
- Formant and Amplitude Transitions are a thing. Only appear when that part of a note is edited.
- Separate note by trills. Move notes into center of two keys. Vibrato trick. Goes back and forth.
### Melodyne: Creative mixing tricks for sibilants [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XoNngoqhUMg)
- Separate Sibilants and consonants into different tracks.
- Lower the volume track of sibilants to control it.
- Can put a high shelf on consonant track without adding harshness.
- Put reverb and effects on track without sibilants.
### Distorted vocals: How to treat and tame them in Melodyne [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PWsvczwST8M)
- Sibilants that are too long are caused by Melodyne being confused. 
- Note Selection Mode: Complex
- Turn on Robust pitch Curve. Double click.
- Can cut notes into pieces to help with detection.
### The biggest Melodyne time savers [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Jr8kxDk3Mo)
- Ctrl + Shift: Click notes to zoom on it.
- Ctrl + Shift: Click background to zoom back out.
### Pitch correction basics in Melodyne essential [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f1NxO0dQ18k)
- Separate note in either edit or note selection mode.
- Can be used to clean up headphone bleed or slight noise.
### Melodyne: Next generation vocal riding [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akpApjt3baY)
- Copy note. Paste it over. Fade in to counteract fade out.
- If change between notes isn't smooth, cut start of drop-off, increase amplitude, adjust transition between the two. Change size of cut blob and transition to create consistency.
### Vocal stability with Melodyne [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6YYaJSX_8Y)
- Time Handle tool can be use to adjust slop by adjusting timing of the note.
- Adjust formants to keep tone. Can move all formants on one line and control the entire thing at once.
### Note-based vocal FX in Melodyne [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvH31Xw5YKU)
- Copy Note. Paste over. Move up and forward slightly. Adjust amplitude. Creates Chorus effects.
- Can create harmonies. Copy notes. Pitch up. Adjust amplitude and other settings. May also adjust notes. May completely swap pitch modulation.
### Advanced vocal editing in Melodyne [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lPXlPZqwY6M)
- Stretch notes out a bit for impact and punch.
- time handles to stretch pitch inside of notes.
- consistency with Amplitude transitions. Cut the blob when it starts to waver. Increase the amp of the right half. Slow Transition. Bring second blob back down. Creates a consistent shape.
- Raise formants for air, lower them for heft.
### Unique Voice Processing with Melodyne [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ITDNj3gHMaQ)
- Play with Formants.
- Adjust Attack speed of Amplitude. Alternatively use a Fade-In.
