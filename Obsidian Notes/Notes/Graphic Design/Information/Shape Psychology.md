- Circles: Unity, Wholeness, Harmony. Comforting, Stable, Protective.
- Square: Stability, Reliability, Professionalism. Order, Structure, and trustworthiness.
- Triangle: Depends on orientation. Up: Stability and Progress. Down: Instability or focus. Right: Movement and Forward Progression
- Rectangle: Sturdy and Dependable. Add directional element. Feel secure and organized.
- Hexagon: Connection and efficiency. Often associated with technology or collaboration. 
- Oval: Motion or the flow of time.
### Organic Shapes
- A modern, open-ended feeling.
### Symbolic Shape Psychology
- Hearts, Starts, Crosses, Arrows.
- Very, very literal.
- Line Based Shapes: Direct focus or create movement and rhythm.
- Vertical Lines are Dominant and Energetic. Horizontal are Peaceful and Stable.
### Tips
- Triangles are the ultimate tool for creating a sense of movement and drawing attention.
- Squares and rectangles can be used for education or informative designs. Grid-based layout example. Rounded corners make it more casual.
- Try to combine shapes with colors that evoke the same emotion.
- Slanted or angled shapes add a sense of movement.
- Freeform organic is perfect for wellness and beauty products.
